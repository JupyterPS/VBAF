# Semester Plan: 12-Week ML Programme

## Programme: Applied Machine Learning for Enterprise IT
Duration: 12 weeks
Sessions: 1 x 3-hour session per week
Assessment: Weekly labs (60%) + Final project (40%)

## Weeks 1-3: Foundations

| Week | Topic | Tutorial |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Neural networks and backpropagation | 01, 02 |
| 2 | Classification and regression | 06, 07 |
| 3 | Loading and preparing real data | 08 |

## Weeks 4-6: ML Engineering

| Week | Topic | Tutorial |
|------|-------|---------|
| 4 | Feature engineering | 09 |
| 5 | Model selection and tuning | 10 |
| 6 | Production pipelines | 11, 03 |

## Weeks 7-9: Reinforcement Learning

| Week | Topic | Tutorial |
|------|-------|---------|
| 7 | Q-Learning fundamentals | 03, 04 |
| 8 | Deep Q-Networks | 12 |
| 9 | Enterprise environments | 05 |

## Weeks 10-11: Enterprise Automation

| Week | Topic | Tutorial |
|------|-------|---------|
| 10 | Enterprise pillars — Phases 14-20 | Case studies |
| 11 | Enterprise pillars — Phases 21-27 | Benchmarks |

## Week 12: Final Project

Students build and present a custom enterprise pillar for a domain
relevant to their organisation. Presentation includes:
- Problem definition
- Signal design and justification
- Training results (3 runs, average improvement)
- Business case for deployment

## Assessment Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Weekly lab completion | 40% |
| Lab code quality | 20% |
| Final project improvement % | 20% |
| Final project presentation | 20% |
